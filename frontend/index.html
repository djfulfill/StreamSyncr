Who We Are

We're builders who got tired of the streaming chaos. Five different tracking apps. Three media servers. Two debrid services. No connection between any of them.

StreamSyncr was born from a simple idea: your data should work for you, not against you. We believe every streamer deserves a unified view of their entertainment — no matter what platforms they use.

We're not a corporation. We're a project built by the community, for the community. Open source. Self-hosted. Your data stays yours.

What StreamSyncr Does

One addon. Every service. Zero compromise.

⚡

Unified Tracking

Trakt, Simkl, AniList, WeTrakr, Letterboxd, IMDb — all your watch history and lists in one place. No more switching apps.

🖥️

Media Server Bridge

Plex, Jellyfin, Kodi — your self-hosted libraries connected and synced. See everything you own alongside your streaming services.

🔗

Debrid Integration

Real-Debrid, TorBox, AllDebrid — instant stream resolution. Click a title, get a link. No more copy-pasting magnets.

📺

30+ Catalogs

Trending, popular, your watchlists, your favorites — all surfaced directly in Stremio. Discovery has never been easier.

📦

Data Export

Export your entire watch history from 9 services as JSON. Your data, your format, your control. Always.

🔒

Self-Hosted

Run it on your own machine. Your tokens, your data, your server. No third party sees your watch history.
